Privacy policy

Applies to the private Google OAuth client named "rclone". Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Who this is for

This OAuth client is created and used by a single individual (the owner) to connect the open-source rclone tool, running on the owner's own devices, to the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the client can read, create, update and delete files and metadata in the owner's own Google Drive, in order to synchronize files between the owner's device and Google Drive.

What is collected, stored, and shared

  • This project operates no servers and collects no data.
  • Files and account information are transferred directly between the owner's device and Google. They are not sent to any other party.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration on the owner's device.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics, or training of AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local rclone configuration removes the stored tokens. No copy of the data is kept anywhere by this project.
