rclone (personal)

This is the home page of a private Google OAuth client named rclone. It is used by one individual, its owner, to synchronize his own files between his Android device and his own Google Drive using the open-source rclone command-line tool.

The client is not a public service, has no sign-up, and is not offered to other people. It has no servers and no website back end: sign-in happens between the owner's device and Google, and the resulting access token is stored only on the owner's device.

Google Drive access

The client requests Google Drive access so that the owner's own sync tool can read, create, update and delete files in the owner's own Drive. No data is collected, stored, sold, or shared by this project.
